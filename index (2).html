About

I like work that starts on a drawing and ends on a bench — machining parts, running airflow tests, or walking a pipeline excavation to check corrosion data against the print.

My internships have moved between energy infrastructure and automotive product engineering: revising gas engineering standards and methane emissions reporting at Consumers Energy, and redesigning a production seat ventilation system at Adient that's projected to save $358K a year. Outside of internships, I lead fabrication on a university aerospace research project and design components for MRacing's Formula SAE chassis team.

I'm comfortable across the full loop: CAD and GD&T in Siemens NX and SolidWorks, machining on a manual mill and lathe, and analysis in MATLAB — plus teaching, as a peer facilitator for 35 students in UROP.
