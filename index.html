Kresling Tube Collapse Study

UM Aerospace Dept. Undergraduate Research — Fabrication & Testing Lead

An origami-inspired tube that collapses in a controlled, twisting motion under compression, designed to absorb and dissipate energy in a lighter, more predictable structure for potential aerospace applications. I led fabrication and experimental testing, connecting structural mechanics and energy absorption to 3D printing, load-frame stress vs. strain analysis, and Digital Image Correlation. Presented at the UROP Symposium with Georgina Sanchez, Colin Hunter, and Royan D'Mello.

Blender model → 3D-printed sample → load frame test setup
Kresling unit geometry (studied by D'Mello & Hunter) and a multi-Kresling stack
